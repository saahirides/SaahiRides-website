On-Demand Pricing

Pay for each trip.

  • City fares use cumulative distance slabs up to 30 km.
  • Each distance slab uses its approved multiplier.
  • For a city trip beyond 30 km, the separate city-to-outstation handoff rules apply.
  • Bike, Scooty, EV, Auto and Cab options have their applicable rates and service tiers.

Subscription Pricing

Plan your regular route for 30 days.

  • Pay 10–15% less than On-Demand prices on eligible regular journeys.
  • Planned automatic eligibility detection after the same fixed-distance trip and time slot are used over 3 days; bot calculations will be added later.
  • Dedicated driver, route lock and no need to rebook the same daily trip manually.
  • Planned automatic ride-balance deduction after each completed ride and a reminder 5 days before plan expiry; these automated calculations and reminders will be added to the bot later.
  • Unused eligible balance is intended to roll over for one month and lapse after 5 days if unused; the bot will enforce these rules later.
  • SOS and ride insurance included at no extra cost, subject to applicable insurance terms.

City Trip Distance Limit & Outstation Handoff

City vehicles cannot be billed with city pricing beyond 30 km. For a city traveller whose trip exceeds 30 km, the city fare calculation stops and the existing SaahiRides outstation pricing rules apply. This special threshold rule does not replace the separate rules for a customer who books an outstation trip from the start.

Actual city-trip distanceBilling treatmentBillable distance
Up to 30 kmCumulative city pricingActual distance
30.01–50 kmExisting outstation pricing50 km minimum
50.01–100 kmExisting outstation pricing100 km minimum

Examples: 35 km → 50 km billed; 50 km → 50 km billed; 51 km → 100 km billed; 80 km → 100 km billed; 100 km → 100 km billed.

The outstation amount is calculated by the existing outstation pricing rules. Do not apply the city slab multipliers to trips over 30 km, and do not use this city-traveller threshold to rewrite normal outstation bookings. For a confirmed itemised quote, contact SaahiRides at 9347946026.
