SaahiRides Subscription Benefits

Subscription totals are calculated from the published On-Demand fares multiplied by the number of planned trip legs, plus the applicable 30-day route-lock fee.

⚙️

Automatic Eligibility

Planned benefit: eligibility can be identified when the same time slot and fixed-distance journey are used over 3 days. Automated eligibility checks will be implemented later in the booking bot.

🤝

Your Dedicated Driver

Enjoy a familiar, dependable driver for your regular route—like a family member dropping you at work or college.

📲

No Daily Rebooking

Once your subscription is set up, you do not need to book the same regular journey through the portal or app every day.

💳

Automatic Ride-Balance Deduction

Planned benefit: ride balance will be deducted after each completed ride once the booking bot supports this calculation.

🔔

Expiry Reminder

Planned benefit: a reminder is intended 5 days before plan expiry; automated reminders will be added with the booking bot.

📍

Route Lock

Lock in your regular route for a more consistent everyday commute.

🔄

Unused Balance Carry-Forward

Planned benefit: unused eligible balance can roll over to the next month and must be used within 5 days of carry-forward. Balance tracking and expiry enforcement will be implemented in the booking bot.

🆘

SOS at No Extra Cost

SOS is available with the subscription at no additional charge.

🛡️

Ride Insurance at No Extra Cost

Ride insurance is available at no additional charge, subject to the applicable coverage terms.

₹

On-Demand Pricing

Pay for each trip.

  • City fares use cumulative distance slabs up to 30 km.
  • Each distance slab uses its approved multiplier.
  • For a city trip beyond 30 km, the separate city-to-outstation handoff rules apply.
  • Bike, Scooty, EV, Auto and Cab options have their applicable rates and service tiers.
View On-Demand Fare Chart ↓
📅

Subscription Pricing

Plan your regular route for 30 days.

  • 30-day one-way total: On-Demand trip fare × 30 + ₹199 route lock.
  • 30-day both-way total: On-Demand trip fare × 60 + ₹299 route lock.
  • Planned automatic eligibility detection after the same fixed-distance trip and time slot are used over 3 days; bot calculations will be added later.
  • Dedicated driver, route lock and no need to rebook the same daily trip manually.
  • Planned automatic ride-balance deduction after each completed ride and a reminder 5 days before plan expiry; these automated calculations and reminders will be added to the bot later.
  • Unused eligible balance is intended to roll over for one month and lapse after 5 days if unused; the bot will enforce these rules later.
  • SOS and ride insurance included at no extra cost, subject to applicable insurance terms.
Ask for Subscription Plans ↗

Important: On-Demand city pricing applies only up to 30 km. The city-traveller threshold is separate from normal outstation bookings. Confirm the applicable vehicle, route, subscription tier and final itemised fare with SaahiRides before booking.

City Trip Distance Limit & Outstation Handoff

City cumulative pricing applies through 30 km. For city-cab trips beyond 30 km, the extended city fare now increases progressively instead of jumping to the 100 km charge at 50.01 km. These city bands do not replace normal outstation booking minimums.

Actual city-trip distanceBilling treatmentRate-equivalent billing basis
Up to 30 kmExisting cumulative city pricingActual progressive city fare
30.01–50 km50 km rate basis × 1.2562.5 rate-equivalent km
50.01–100 kmIncrease linearly from 62.5 to 150 rate-equivalent km62.5 + (actual km − 50) × 1.75
Above 100 kmMove to separately approved outstation policyOutstation tariff and minimums apply

Illustrative rate-equivalent examples: 50 km → 62.5; 51 km → 64.25; 75 km → 106.25; 80 km → 115; 100 km → 150. Multiply this equivalent by the applicable cab's approved per-km base rate. This keeps the fare continuous at 50 km and avoids a sudden 50-to-51 km jump.

Scope: This extended city-cab calculation applies to Non-AC and AC cabs only, not bikes, scooties, Scooty EV, Auto Rickshaw or Electric Auto Rickshaw. Above 100 km, normal outstation rules apply, including 350 km minimum one-way or 300 km/day minimum round trip, calculated Hyderabad garage-to-garage. Night charges apply from 10:00 pm to 6:00 am.
