CEO Corner

Building journeys around trust

A note from Subodh Kumar Dandapat, Founder & CEO — SaahiRides Transit Pvt. Ltd.

SaahiRides was built around a simple belief: everyday mobility should feel dependable, transparent and respectful of both passengers and driver-partners.

Predictability matters

People plan their day around a ride. They deserve clarity about the service they are choosing, the price they are being quoted and the support available if something goes wrong. We want our communication to be as straightforward as our service promise.

Safety is operational, not a slogan

Trust has to be earned through clear processes, responsible driver onboarding, reliable support and honest descriptions of what is—and is not—available. We will not describe a feature as active until it has been tested.

Growing responsibly

We are building SaahiRides step by step, listening to customers and driver-partners, and improving the experience before expanding our promises. Thank you to everyone who has encouraged this journey.

Subodh Kumar Dandapat
Founder & CEO
