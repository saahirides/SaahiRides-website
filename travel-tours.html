Extra charges: GST and travel insurance coverage are extra. The customer will be told the price before confirmation. Driver food, driver bata/allowance and toll charges are included in the package unless the written quote expressly states otherwise.

Before paying: ask for the written itinerary, exact pickup and drop times, total package price, included meals/stay/tickets, add-on prices, and cancellation terms. Any change to the itinerary will be communicated by the SaahiRides team.

Travel Operations, Safety & Billing

SaahiRides uses a written trip plan, a driver trip sheet, customer safety rules and a transparent bill for every confirmed outstation journey.

1. Outstation distance & extra-kilometre ratesPer vehicle, per day

Minimum billable distance: 300 km per vehicle per day. Record the actual odometer reading and route every day. If actual distance exceeds 300 km that day, the excess distance is charged at the applicable vehicle rate below.

Vehicle categoryExtra distance above 300 km/day
Sedan₹18/km
Premium₹22/km
Innova (7-seater)₹26/km

Billing example: if a vehicle covers 340 km in a day, the excess is 40 km. The extra-distance charge is 40 × the applicable rate. The base package/vehicle charge, taxes and any separately agreed items must appear as separate lines in the written quote. Do not treat the excess-kilometre rate as the full package price.

The final quote must state how garage-to-garage kilometres are measured and which day counts as a billing day. Every assigned vehicle must have a working FASTag with sufficient balance checked before departure. Drivers must record toll transactions and retain receipts if FASTag fails and an alternate payment is required. Toll charges, driver food and driver bata/allowance are included in the package. GST and travel insurance coverage are extra. Parking, permits, interstate charges or any other exclusions must be clearly disclosed in the written quote before payment; no unlisted charge should be added without disclosure and agreement.

2. Mandatory driver trip sheetComplete every trip day

The driver must complete the trip sheet before departure, update it during the trip and close it with the customer or operations team at the end of each day.

  • Booking ID, package name, date, driver name/contact and vehicle registration/category.
  • Customer/group lead name and approved contact; passenger count and agreed pickup/drop points.
  • Scheduled and actual reporting, pickup, departure, stop, arrival and drop-off times.
  • Start/end odometer readings, daily kilometres, approved route and any extra-distance calculation.
  • FASTag active and valid, sufficient balance checked before departure, toll transactions recorded, and receipts retained if a FASTag issue requires alternate payment. Toll charges are included in the package and must not be charged to the customer again.
  • Meal breaks, night-halt location, accommodation confirmation and planned next-day departure.
  • Included services delivered, optional add-ons approved by the customer, and itemised receipts/charges.
  • Delays, route changes, vehicle checks, incidents, complaints, SOS/emergency actions and who was informed.
  • Customer acknowledgement, driver sign-off and operations review at trip closure.

Do not record unnecessary sensitive personal information on a trip sheet. Store completed sheets only in the approved company system and share them only with authorised staff.

3. Customer do’s & don’tsFor a safe, smooth journey

DO

  • Be ready at the confirmed pickup point and time; keep the booking confirmation available.
  • Wear seat belts where provided, follow lawful safety instructions and supervise children.
  • Respect temple, hotel, wildlife-park and local rules; follow the agreed meeting time at stops.
  • Confirm any optional upgrade or extra service and its price with operations before accepting it.
  • Report delays, safety concerns, lost property or service issues to the official SaahiRides contact.

DON’T

  • Do not ask the driver to speed, drive while fatigued or break traffic/parking rules.
  • Do not smoke, carry illegal items or damage the vehicle/accommodation.
  • Do not assume VIP/special darshan, attraction tickets, private rooms or extra activities are included unless written in the confirmed package.
  • Do not make unapproved route changes or request chargeable add-ons without agreeing the cost.
  • Do not pay unofficial charges without requesting an itemised receipt; contact operations if a billing dispute arises.
4. Driver training & trip-readiness requirementsBefore assignment
  • Verified identity, valid driving licence, required permits and completed company onboarding/KYC.
  • Defensive driving, traffic rules, hill-road/night-driving risk awareness and fatigue/rest management.
  • Pre-trip vehicle inspection: tyres, brakes, lights, seat belts, documents, fuel/charge, cleanliness and emergency equipment.
  • Confirm the vehicle has a working FASTag with sufficient balance before departure; know how to report a failed tag and retain toll receipts if alternate payment is needed.
  • Customer service, punctuality, professional conduct, privacy, respectful communication and no harassment.
  • Women/child passenger safety, approved route discipline, SOS escalation and emergency contact procedure.
  • First-aid/emergency basics, breakdown response, incident reporting and safe passenger evacuation.
  • Trip-sheet completion, odometer-based billing, approved expense handling, receipts and customer handover.
  • Package-specific briefing covering itinerary, meal/stay arrangements, darshan or attraction rules, pickup/drop schedule and escalation contact.
