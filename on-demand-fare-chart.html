Progressive cumulative fare rules: The base fare covers the first 3 km, calculated as 3 × the selected vehicle and service tier's per-kilometre rate. Each multiplier applies only to kilometres within its own slab, and the slab fares are added cumulatively: 3–5 km at 1.15×; 5–10 km at 1.25×; 10–15 km at 1.50×; 15–20 km at 1.75×; 20–25 km at 2.00×; and 25–30 km at 2.25×.

Scope: Extended distance billing above 30 km is for Non-AC and AC city cabs only. It does not apply to bikes, scooties, Scooty EV, Auto Rickshaw or Electric Auto Rickshaw.

Night charges: Applicable from 10:00 pm to 6:00 am. The night-charge amount is not specified on this page and must follow the approved SaahiRides tariff.

Separate outstation tariff reference: Sedan ₹18/km one-way or ₹16/km round-trip; Ertiga ₹22/km one-way or ₹20/km round-trip; Innova/Crysta ₹25/km one-way or ₹24/km round-trip. These are separate outstation rates and should not be confused with the 50 km / 100 km city-cab billing bands.

Rate notes: *Bike Super Fast and Auto Super Fast rates are provisional and require confirmation. Scooty EV and Electric Auto Express/Super Fast rates are not yet confirmed. Unconfirmed rates must not be assumed or automatically filled in.
