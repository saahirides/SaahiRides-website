From the founder's desk

Archived CEO / Owner & Co-founder Message

Welcome to SaahiRides Transit Pvt. Ltd. SaahiRides began with a simple purpose: make everyday travel safer, clearer and more affordable for the people who depend on it. We want customers to feel respected, drivers to be treated fairly, and every journey to be guided by responsibility.

“Your Journey, Our Responsibility. SafeRide @ SaahiPrice.”

Our promise is to keep pricing transparent, avoid surge pricing, offer practical ride choices and continue improving the customer experience. The company’s journey is built step by step—with care, accountability and the trust of the community.

People & culture

Archived HR Message

At SaahiRides, people are central to the journey. Our customers deserve courteous service and a safe experience; our drivers and team members deserve dignity, clear expectations and a fair opportunity to grow.

We stand for Safety First, respectful conduct, confidentiality, responsible service and a workplace where concerns can be raised and handled seriously. We expect every member of the SaahiRides family to help build trust through professionalism and care.

Where we want to go

SaahiRides Vision

Our vision is to make dependable, accessible and fairly priced mobility a practical choice for everyday people—not just for occasional journeys.

  • Put safety, respect and customer confidence first.
  • Keep fares clear and avoid surge pricing.
  • Build a dependable network of verified, professional drivers.
  • Offer useful mobility choices, including bikes, scooties, electric vehicles, autos, cabs, rentals and outstation travel.
  • Use technology and thoughtful service to make regular journeys simpler.
  • Create sustainable opportunities for drivers and a trusted local transport brand.
