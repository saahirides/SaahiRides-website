Journey at a glance

Starting point
Hyderabad, Telangana

Isha Yoga Center, Velliangiri Foothills, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu

Recommended duration
Plan 3 nights / 4 days or longer by road; do not rush the long drive.

Travel safety: Hyderabad–Coimbatore is a long road journey (roughly 900 km one way, depending on route). If travelling with children or driving alone, plan rest stops and an overnight halt en route. Do not plan the entire circuit and return drive in two days.

Suggested itinerary

Day 1

Hyderabad departure

Depart at a sensible time, take meal and fuel breaks, and halt overnight on the route. Actual route and stop depend on traffic and driver condition.

Day 2

Isha Yoga Center

Reach Coimbatore after rest, check in as per the cottage's confirmed timing, and plan Adiyogi, Dhyanalinga and Linga Bhairavi around current visitor timings and rules. If arriving late, keep the visit light.

Day 3

Vellore extension

Travel to Vellore (roughly 360–395 km from Coimbatore by road; allow around 5½–7 hours or more). Stay overnight in Vellore rather than driving tired.

Day 4

Golden Temple & shopping

Visit Sripuram Golden Temple, plan lunch subject to current arrangements, set aside 2–3 hours for saree/dress shopping, then plan the return journey with a suitable overnight halt if needed.

This is a planning framework, not a confirmed booking or a guarantee of entry, show timings, meal access or accommodation. If only two days are available in total, make Vellore a separate trip.

Isha Yoga Center: visit planning

  • Adiyogi (112-foot statue): allow time for the grounds and any scheduled evening programme.
  • Dhyanalinga: quiet meditation space; follow silence, dress and phone instructions.
  • Linga Bhairavi: check the current shrine schedule before setting the day's order.
  • Adiyogi Divya Darshanam: verify the show is scheduled on your visit date and confirm the time locally.

General visitor access to key spaces is commonly described as free, but specific programmes, offerings and services may carry charges. Follow the centre's current instructions.

Vellore: darshan, sarees and food

  • Sripuram Golden Temple: confirm current darshan timings, entry instructions, footwear and phone/photography rules on the official site.
  • Saree and dress shopping: allow 2–3 hours to compare silk sarees, ethnic wear and dress materials. Request a proper invoice and check silk descriptions/certification where relevant.
  • Local food: choose a family restaurant based on current opening hours and dietary preferences; Arcot makkan peda is a regional sweet to look for.
  • Lunch: check temple food/annadanam availability and serving times rather than assuming it is available every day to every visitor.

Before you travel — checklist

  • Confirm cottage booking, check-in/check-out and meal eligibility.
  • Check visitor timings and programme schedule shortly before departure.
  • Carry identification, medicines, water and comfortable walking footwear.
  • Follow mobile/photography restrictions in designated spiritual spaces.
  • Plan safe rest stops; never drive while fatigued.
  • For families, confirm child occupancy, room bedding and any age-related charges.
