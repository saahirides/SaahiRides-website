Attractions & experiences

  • List attractions in practical route order and state when each visit is optional.
  • Include estimated route kilometres, travel time and planned rest stops.
  • Explain accommodation, meals, tickets and activities that are included or extra.
  • Add verified photos, relevant video links and seasonal or access notes where useful.

Customer-ready checklist

  • Day-by-day schedule with realistic timings and travel distances
  • Vehicle and passenger details; pickup, drop and night-halt information
  • Itemised quotation, GST, inclusions/exclusions and payment/cancellation policy
  • Safety guidance, customer do’s and don’ts, feedback and Google review link
  • WhatsApp enquiry action and a clear note that final plans depend on confirmation
