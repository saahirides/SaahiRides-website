Sample Bengaluru + Mysuru family itinerary

Day 1 · Bengaluru heritage
Visit Bangalore Palace, Tipu Sultan’s Summer Palace and Vidhana Soudha exterior, subject to access and timing.
Day 2 · Gardens and discovery
Explore Cubbon Park, the Bal Bhavan area, Lalbagh or the Visvesvaraya Industrial and Technological Museum. Bannerghatta Biological Park can be planned as a separate outing.
Day 3 · Transfer to Mysuru
Take the planned Bengaluru–Mysuru highway transfer, then visit Mysore Palace and St. Philomena’s Cathedral depending on arrival time.
Day 4 · Mysuru and nearby attractions
Choose Chamundi Hills, Mysore Zoo, Srirangapatna, Ranganathittu or Brindavan Gardens based on opening times and the agreed route.

Sample only. We will adjust the number of days, attractions, kilometres, rest breaks, accommodation and final drop-off after receiving your trip details. Avoid squeezing every attraction into a single day.
