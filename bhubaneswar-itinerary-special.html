1. Bhubaneswar heritage circuit

Bhubaneswar is known as the Temple City, with layers of Kalinga architecture and archaeological history. Give the temple precincts and cave hills unhurried time rather than trying to tick off every stop quickly.

Lingaraj Temple

A major Shiva temple and a landmark of Kalinga architecture. Entry restrictions apply to non-Hindus; visitors who cannot enter should plan an exterior view from the permitted public viewing area. Photography rules around the temple must be respected.

Udayagiri Caves

Explore the ancient Jain rock-cut caves, inscriptions and sculptural details. Rani Gumpha is a prominent highlight. Wear comfortable footwear and take care on uneven stone steps.

Khandagiri Caves

Visit the neighbouring hill's cave complexes and viewpoints. Allow time for steps, the terrain and photography from permitted areas.

Optional temple additions

Mukteswar, Rajarani and Parasurameswar temples can be added when time and opening hours permit. Dhauli Shanti Stupa is a separate extension and should not be squeezed into a tight cave-and-temple circuit.

2. Nandankanan Zoological Park — a family wildlife day

Set aside several hours for the zoological park and its visitor experiences. Check the official park website for the current weekly closure, entry tickets, safari operations, timings, visitor rules and any seasonal restrictions before confirming the day. Do not promise a particular animal sighting or safari slot.

Zoo exploration

Plan a relaxed walking route, water breaks and rest stops, especially for children and senior travellers.

Safari and other experiences

Include only the safari or visitor experiences operating and bookable on the travel date. Ticketing and availability are subject to park rules.

3. Taste of Odisha — the BBSR food trail

Food is part of the destination. Build in time for local breakfast, street snacks and a traditional sweet stop instead of treating these dishes as a passing mention.

Bara, samosa & ghugni

A satisfying local breakfast or snack combination. Try bara with ghugni, and include samosa as a separate tea-time option at a hygienic local vendor.

Dahibara Aloodum

Odia street-food favourite: soft bara soaked in seasoned curd, served with spicy aloodum and toppings. Cuttack is especially associated with this dish; include a well-chosen local stop.

Salepur Rasagulla

Make room for the famous Salepur chhena sweet as a dedicated dessert stop. The Salepur/Cuttack-side visit is an optional route extension; confirm the shop and opening hours before departure.

Food-stop planning: Vendor names, food hygiene, opening hours and availability must be checked locally. The itinerary does not imply a partnership or endorsement by any restaurant or sweet shop.

4. Suggested two-day BBSR itinerary

Day 1 — Temple City, caves & Odia flavours

7:00 AM: Breakfast with bara, ghugni and samosa. 8:00 AM: Lingaraj Temple visit or permitted exterior viewing. 10:30 AM: Udayagiri Caves. 12:00 PM: Khandagiri Caves. 1:30 PM: Lunch and a Dahibara Aloodum stop. Afternoon: Rest or add Mukteswar/Rajarani if time and opening hours permit. Evening: Local market and snacks.

Day 2 — Nandankanan wildlife experience

Morning: Breakfast and travel to Nandankanan. Daytime: Spend several hours at the zoo and any safari experiences operating that day. Afternoon: Lunch and a relaxed return. Optional extension: Add Salepur for rasagulla only if the route, traffic and onward departure make the detour practical; otherwise keep it as a separate Cuttack-side food excursion.

This split protects the zoo visit from being rushed and avoids forcing Salepur into an already full sightseeing day. Actual order may change with zoo closure days, temple access, weather and the group's pace.
