Vellore: plan a meaningful visit

A practical starting point for travellers considering Vellore, whether the journey is for heritage, education, healthcare or a wider Tamil Nadu itinerary.

Plan the journey before you leave

Start by confirming your travel date, pickup point, number of passengers and luggage. If you are connecting from another city, allow a realistic buffer for transfers and local traffic.

Build your day around your priorities

Decide which places matter most to your group and check current opening hours, entry rules and local transport arrangements directly before departure. Keep the itinerary flexible enough for breaks and unexpected delays.

What to confirm before booking

  • Pickup and drop locations, including any intermediate stops
  • Vehicle category and passenger/luggage capacity
  • Expected journey duration and rest stops
  • Any tickets, accommodation or meals that must be arranged separately
