Fare rules at a glance

First 3 km are calculated at the selected per-kilometre rate. Additional kilometres are calculated progressively within each slab: 3–5 km × 1.15; 5–10 km × 1.25; 10–15 km × 1.50; 15–20 km × 1.75; 20–25 km × 2.00; 25–30 km × 2.25.

Subscription savings are intended to be 10–15% below comparable On-Demand prices for eligible regular journeys. Only confirmed subscription prices are displayed as fixed amounts; unconfirmed prices are not guessed.
