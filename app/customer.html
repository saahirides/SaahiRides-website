SaahiRides Passenger Promise & Ride Charter — Pillar #4

Brand Ambassador Dignity, Zero-Tipping & 24-Hour Customer KYC Verification

Our drivers are our brand ambassadors. Kindly treat every SaahiRides partner with full dignity and respect. Tipping is not expected; misconduct, harassment or inhumane treatment is not acceptable.

Zero-Argument Policy: Please do not argue with drivers. For any trip or billing concern, contact SaahiRides Support immediately on +91 93479 46026 so the matter can be handled through the support process.

Customer Identity & 24-Hour KYC Validation Window: Kindly allow SaahiRides up to 24 hours to verify your profile and service eligibility, particularly for new accounts, subscriptions, outstation trips and self-drive/rental bookings. You may be asked to show valid identity proof before boarding or during your journey. Verification requests will be handled through the applicable service process and privacy notice.

Ready to Book a Ride?

Registration is not a booking confirmation. Send your pickup and destination to SaahiRides to begin a booking request. Your ride is confirmed only after availability and eligibility checks.
